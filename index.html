Conversion1 CCF = 748.05 gal
SPU rates (this bill)Base $47.67 · Summer $5.98 and $7.39 per CCF · Winter $5.82 per CCF · Sewer $20.18 per CCF
  • Base charge: split in equal thirds, since it is owed regardless of use.
  • Water usage: (water total − base) × each unit's share of the billed CCF.
  • Sewer: total × each unit's water-usage share (SPU bills sewer on a fixed CCF, so water share is the proxy).
  • Unit usage: average gal/day from that unit's readings × days in the bill period ÷ 748.05.
  • Unit without a reading: assigned the remainder of the billed CCF.
